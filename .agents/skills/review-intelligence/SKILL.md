# Review Intelligence Skill

## Purpose
Extracts customer feedback patterns, recurring complaints, and standout strengths to understand business reputation.

## Rules
- Do not invent quotes or review counts.
- Distinguish observed statistics from AI sentiment inferences.
- Attach evidence items to sentiment summaries.
